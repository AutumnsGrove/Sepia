Build the draft card from exact quotes copied from her messages. It saves nothing: she confirms the card.
It refuses a body that is not her own words, and it never sets a rating the service did not read.

completion says which shelf the card is on: want_to, finished or abandoned. Leave it out to keep the shelf
the service already read from her words. A wanted work needs only the work, an abandoned one needs a body
and no rating, and a finished one needs a rating. Her words are welcome on a finished card and are not
required: call it with no quotes when she has none, and the card is a rated entry with nothing written.

progress_quote is how far she got, quoted from her message ("about 100 pages in", "two episodes"). It is
only used on an abandoned card, and it is refused if it is not exactly her words. Never write one.
