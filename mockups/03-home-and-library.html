Home, drafts and library

Home is a launcher, not a chat: the composer sits where the thumb is, and anything above it is one tap from where you left off. Reading happens on plain lists, with no cards and no clutter.

9. Searching your words

Search spans the finished and stopped shelves, because both hold words. A stopped row names its shelf where the stars would be. A wanted work has no words, so it is found by title only and is not part of a "mentions" count.
